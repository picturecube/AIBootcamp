Common web browsers include: 🌐 Google Chrome 🌐 Microsoft Edge 🌐 Mozilla Firefox 🌐 Safari (on Mac computers) **Step 1: Turn On Your Computer** Find the **Power** button on your computer. Look carefully for one of these icons: 🌐 Google Chrome 🌐 Microsoft Edge 🌐 Mozilla Firefox 🌐 Safari (Mac) The icon might be: On the desktop On the taskbar at the bottom of the screen Inside the Start menu (Windows) Inside the Applications folder (Mac) Take your time. There is no need to rush. **Step 3: Open the Web Browser** Move the mouse slowly. Watch the mouse pointer move on the screen. Move the pointer until it is directly on top of the browser icon. Stop moving the mouse. Press the **left mouse button once**. Remove your finger from the mouse button. Wait a few seconds. A browser window will open. **Step 4: Find the Address Bar** Look at the very top of the browser window. You will see a long box. This is called the **address bar**. Each result usually includes: A title A short description The website address Read the list slowly. Choose the result that looks most helpful. Press the **left mouse button once**. Remove your finger from the mouse. Wait while the webpage opens. The webpage will appear. **Step 10: Read the Webpage** Read the information at your own pace. Wait for your home page to appear. **Step 19: Visit Another Website** Whenever you want to visit another website: Move the mouse to the address bar. Click once. Wait for the blinking cursor.
